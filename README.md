# RecycleLink ♻️

**Connecting waste collectors with authorized recyclers for a smarter, more sustainable future.**

## 🌍 About

RecycleLink is a digital platform designed to bridge the gap between informal waste collectors and authorized recycling facilities.

It aims to make waste recycling more accessible, transparent, and rewarding by helping collectors discover recyclers, compare prices, manage waste listings, and maintain transparent transaction records.

## 🎯 The Problem

Informal waste collectors are an essential part of the recycling ecosystem, yet they often face challenges such as:

- Lack of transparent and fair market pricing.
- Difficulty finding authorized recycling facilities.
- Limited opportunities to compare recycler offers.
- Lack of documented and traceable transactions.
- Limited access to proper waste-handling and safety information.

## 💡 Our Solution

RecycleLink aims to create a reliable digital ecosystem where waste collectors and authorized recyclers can connect, communicate, and conduct more transparent recycling transactions.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Recycler Discovery:** Find authorized recyclers based on location and accepted waste categories.
- **Waste Listings:** Upload waste details, including material type, quantity, weight, and photographs.
- **Transparent Pricing:** Compare available prices and recycler offers.
- **Smart Matching:** Connect collectors with suitable recyclers based on location, material, and pricing.
- **Digital Transactions:** Maintain records of waste handovers and transactions.
- **Earnings Dashboard:** Track collection activity and earnings.
- **Safety Guidance:** Access practical waste-handling and safety information.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To empower waste collectors, encourage responsible recycling, and contribute to a more transparent and sustainable circular economy.
